# Vela · Season 1 — "The Brochure Tour" (route proposal)

**Status: proposal.** Twelve weeks, eleven cities, twelve pins, 28 Sept –
20 Dec 2026. Every stop is anchored to a real, dated public event —
verified against official sources on 2026-09-04; the two dates still
marked LIKELY are recurring annual events whose 2026 pages post closer to
the day. The route locks only when the operator locks it, and it is
launch-date-sensitive: the anchors are fixed dates, so if gates 0–2
(`docs/vela-production.md` §8) slip past ~Sept 21, weeks 1–2 lose Paris
Fashion Week and the Céline residency and the front of the route gets
recut. **The identity-file go-date is the schedule.**

Why a route is event-led and not city-led: the `travel-places` harvest is
Vela's real-calendar feed, and a "Vela at the thing" post ships *while the
thing trends*. Picking pretty cities first and hoping for events is the
influencer-simulacrum method; picking the autumn's real calendar and
routing through it is ours.

## The season in three acts

- **Act 1 · The Marketed North (Oct)** — fashion week, arena tours, light
  festivals, the year's biggest party conference: peak marketing season,
  reviewed at the source.
- **Act 2 · The Edges (Nov)** — Istanbul, Andalusia, Marrakech: the cities
  the brochures flatten hardest, plus America shipping its sport across an
  ocean twice.
- **Act 3 · The Cosiness Industrial Complex (Dec)** — Christmas markets,
  light festivals, mulled-wine pricing, and the map closing where it
  opened.

Lisbon pins twice — deliberately. Same city, two different things worth
feeling, which is the season's argument made on the map itself.

**Travel legs** stay inside the fiction's physics: Berlin→Amsterdam,
Madrid→Seville and Vienna→Prague are trains (transit is her habitat —
`visual_grammar.recurring_world`); Seville→Marrakech is the short hop it
really is; six flights total, none transatlantic.

## What was considered and passed on (so nobody re-litigates)

- **Madrid, Shakira's 12-night residency at the purpose-built "Estadio
  Shakira" (Oct 2–11)** — the only European stop, and it collides head-on
  with Paris FW + Céline. Paris won: the fashion anchor serves the
  clothing/beauty positioning and the brand-collab ambition; Céline is the
  stronger soften-peak. The stadium-named-after-her material is noted for
  a caption, not a visit.
- **Web Summit Lisbon (Nov 9–12)** — a 70k-lanyard tech conference is
  off-area for a travel-culture critic; the return-to-Lisbon beat was
  spent on the finale instead, where it earns the map.
- **Rome Film Fest, Tokyo IFF, Mexico City (F1 + Día de Muertos + Ed
  Sheeran's world-tour finale Dec 11–12), NYC (100th Macy's parade,
  Rockefeller tree)** — Rome/Tokyo collide with stronger weeks;
  the transatlantic options break the one-suitcase fiction's spine and
  the budget for a 2-day twist. All four are season-2 openers, not
  season-1 detours. The Ed Sheeran finale ("I end my season where he ends
  his world tour") was the hardest cut.
- **Budapest Advent Bazilika** (LIKELY ~Nov 13) — swap bench for Prague
  if Prague's 2026 dates move; multi-year "Europe's best market" winner.

## Production notes for this route

- **Soften-peaks are scheduled, not improvised**: Céline (wk 2) and the
  finale map-close (wk 12) are THE two all-season emotional beats; wk 5's
  marathon dawn is the only mid-season candidate. Everything else stays
  sharp. Hero-clip spend (≤2/month, `docs/vela-production.md` §3) maps to:
  Oct → Céline, Nov → Bernabéu or Jemaa el-Fna at dusk, Dec → the map
  moment.
- **`content.timezone` moves five times**: Lisbon → Paris/Berlin/Amsterdam
  (CET) → Istanbul (TRT) → CET (Madrid onward) → WET (Lisbon finale). The
  scheduled-release windows (§6) make this mechanical; until they land the
  operator times approvals by the city clock.
- **Real-event guardrails apply at every anchor**: crowd, queue and
  production are the material — no artist's face, no identifying stage
  design, no readable signage (`visual_grammar.avoid`). At the NFL games
  and the marathon, athletes are crowd, not subjects.
- **LIKELY dates to re-verify in October**: Prague market opening (posted
  by the organizer in autumn) and nothing else on the critical path —
  every other anchor is already official.
- **If launch slips two weeks**: drop stops 1–2, open in Berlin Oct 12,
  and give Lisbon three finale weeks. The route survives; the fashion
  anchor doesn't.
